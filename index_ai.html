How to Play

Movement
W / Up Arrow — Move Up
S / Down Arrow — Move Down
A / Left Arrow — Move Left
D / Right Arrow — Move Right

Bomb
Spacebar — Place a bomb (explodes in 5 seconds)
Explosion affects 1 block in each direction

Items (hidden in walls)
💔 Broken Heart — Lose a heart
💣 TNT — Double explosion range
🥶 Ice Cube — Frozen for 5 seconds

Tips
Avoid dogs! Touching one costs a heart.
Game over when all 3 hearts are gone.
